JUNE'S JOURNEY — FEATURE

Evergreen Completion

← Back to June's Journey

Evergreen decorations are those that available all the time, regardless of any live or seasonal events going on. We didn't have very many at global launch, so, as I had already taken ownership of the seasonal events, I put forward a proposal to "complete" the evergreen offerings.

Feature Breakdown

Decoration Efficieny

Decorations are part of the core loop for progression. Coins from playing levels are spent on decorations to earn flowers that unlock new levels. A critical gate on this loop, though, is that decorations take up space on the player's island, and the amount of space the player has is limited by progression. Therefore we had to make sure that the player could earn enough flowers within the space they had in order to progress.

The efficiency of flowers per grid space increased time. There were 5 distinct groupings, which, in theory, would let the player feel a boost in progression at the start of the group, and then gradually sense the tightening of space near the end of the group.

The problem was that most items of groups 2-5 were not created. Seasonal decorations would help the player reach around chapter 50 of progression, but beyond that, the player would eventually run out of space to unlock the next level, before they could unlock more space, even if they used the most efficient decoration item available. To verify this, I also mapped out exactly where the pinch points would happen in a data sheet.

The Missing Evergreens

When I joined the team, we had a set amount of evergreen decorations already configured. These were enough to cover player progression to about chapter 20 out of 100. The seasonal decoration events were balanced at the middle efficiency level, so they would be very attractive purchases to new players, and more advanced players could still progress while purchasing them. But we desperately needed the items for group 4 and 5, and also wanted to fill in the missing items from groups 2-3 so they could provide the player with fully realized decoration sets.

With the release of 1 chapter per week, we had roughly 50 weeks before players would start to become blocks (it was actually less time since I believe we global launched with around 20 chapters and players could progress more quickly than 1 chapter per week).

The Plan

Feeling responsible for matters concerning decorations, I asked for time to put together a plan to address this upcoming issue. I was given the go ahead to put together the plan for asset creation and balancing. I was given 1 month to put together the balanced list. The content covered was planned to be created over a year.

Since there were a substantial number of new assets to create, I also proposed creation priority of the assets, so we could provide just the right content in time to let players continue to progress unhindered.

Balancing

While we had a framework for the different efficiency groupings, after spending more time working out the efficiency requirements for each group and the build timers across the entire progression, I proposed some changes to better scale across all the progression. This meant moving the unlock of some of the earlier items to include them in a more appropriate group, and timer changes across all items. For example, since new chapters released weekly, the highest level decorations could take about a week long to complete construction without the player falling behind the release pace.

Results

In the end, the plan was accepted and and followed through with. Over the course of the year I worked with the team to oversee progress versus the release plan and implement newly created assets. I also worked with our marketing and social team to announce and tease new releases.

Ultimately, players were able to progress without being blocked if they continued to build with the higher efficiency items. We avoided any steep retention dropoffs and our retention remained stable.
